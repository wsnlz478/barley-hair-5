FAQ Guide

Everything You Need To Know

Get answers to all your questions about hair restoration procedures, recovery, results, and more

What is hair transplantation?
Hair transplantation is a surgical procedure that moves healthy hair follicles from a donor area (usually the back and sides of the head) to areas experiencing hair loss. These transplanted hairs are genetically resistant to balding and will continue to grow naturally for the rest of your life.
What causes hair loss?
The most common cause is androgenetic alopecia (male or female pattern baldness), which is genetic and hormonal. Other causes include stress, medical conditions, medications, nutritional deficiencies, and aging. During your consultation, we'll help determine the cause of your specific hair loss.
Who is a good candidate for hair transplantation?
Good candidates are men and women with stable hair loss, enough donor hair, realistic expectations, and good overall health. We recommend waiting until your hair loss has stabilized before considering a transplant. We'll assess your candidacy during your free consultation.
What is the difference between FUE and FUT?
FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) involves extracting individual follicles, leaving tiny dots that heal quickly. FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) removes a strip of tissue, which is then divided into grafts. We primarily use our advanced microneedle FUE technique for minimal scarring and faster recovery.
Is the procedure painful?
No! We use local anesthesia to numb the scalp completely. Most patients report feeling very little discomfort during the procedure. You can watch movies, listen to music, or even take a nap while we work.
How long does the procedure take?
The procedure duration depends on the number of grafts being transplanted. Typically, it takes 4-8 hours. We'll give you a precise estimate during your consultation based on your specific treatment plan.
Will I be awake during the procedure?
Yes, you'll be awake but completely comfortable. We use local anesthesia to numb your scalp. You can watch TV, listen to music, take breaks, and even sleep during the procedure. Our team will make sure you're comfortable throughout.
What is microneedle technology?
Our patented microneedle technology uses a specialized implant pen that allows 360° direction control for natural hair growth patterns. The incisions are much smaller (0.6-1.0mm), resulting in faster recovery, less trauma, and higher graft survival rates.
Do I need to shave my head?
Not necessarily! We offer our innovative no-shave hair transplant option. We can discuss your preferences during consultation and recommend the best approach for your specific situation.
What happens during the consultation?
During your free consultation, we'll assess your hair loss, discuss your goals, explain your options, create a personalized treatment plan, and answer all your questions. We'll also provide a transparent cost estimate.
How long is the recovery period?
With our microneedle FUE technique, most patients return to desk work within 1-3 days. Complete healing takes about 1-2 weeks. We'll provide you with detailed aftercare instructions to ensure smooth recovery.
When can I wash my hair?
With our advanced microneedle technique, most patients can gently wash their hair within 24 hours after the procedure. We'll show you the proper washing technique during your post-op care.
When can I exercise?
We recommend avoiding strenuous exercise for the first 10-14 days. Light walking is fine after 3 days. We'll give you specific guidelines based on your procedure and healing progress.
Will there be scarring?
With our microneedle FUE technique, scarring is minimal. The tiny dots heal completely and are virtually undetectable, especially when your hair grows back.
What aftercare is required?
We'll provide you with detailed aftercare instructions including how to wash your hair, medications to use, activities to avoid, and follow-up appointments. Our team is always available if you have questions during recovery.
When will I see results?
You'll start seeing new hair growth at 3-4 months. Visible progress is typically seen at 6 months, and maximum results are achieved at 12-18 months after the procedure.
Are the results permanent?
Yes! The transplanted hair is taken from areas genetically resistant to hair loss. These hairs will continue to grow naturally for the rest of your life.
Will the results look natural?
Absolutely! Our doctors specialize in creating natural-looking hairlines and use 360° direction control for completely natural growth patterns. We also pay close attention to density and distribution for the most natural appearance possible.
What is the graft survival rate?
With our advanced techniques and careful handling of grafts, we achieve graft survival rates of 95% or higher. This is significantly higher than traditional methods.
Can I have more than one procedure?
Yes! Many patients choose to have a second procedure for additional density or coverage. If you have enough donor hair, multiple procedures are completely safe and effective.
How much does hair transplantation cost?
The cost depends on the number of grafts needed and your specific treatment plan. We provide transparent pricing during your free consultation, and we also offer flexible payment options.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes! We offer flexible payment plans to make your hair restoration more affordable. We'll discuss your options during your consultation.
What is included in the price?
Our price includes the procedure itself, all medications needed, follow-up appointments, and aftercare support. We'll provide a full breakdown during your consultation.
Is hair transplant covered by insurance?
In most cases, hair transplantation is considered an elective cosmetic procedure and is not covered by insurance. However, you should check with your insurance provider for your specific policy.
Are consultations free?
Yes! All consultations are completely free and come with no obligation. We want you to have all the information you need to make the best decision for you.
Do you treat international patients?
Absolutely! We welcome patients from around the world. We have English-speaking staff and specialized services for international patients.
Can I have a consultation online?
Yes! We offer free online consultations for international patients. Contact us via WeChat or WhatsApp to schedule your virtual consultation.
How long do I need to stay in China?
We recommend staying for 3-7 days for the procedure and initial follow-up. We'll help you plan your trip and can arrange accommodations if needed.
Do you help with travel arrangements?
Yes! We can assist with arranging accommodations, airport transfers, and provide guidance for your trip. We'll make your visit as smooth and comfortable as possible.
What languages do you speak?
We speak English fluently, and many of our staff also speak other languages. We'll make sure you have clear communication throughout your journey with us.
