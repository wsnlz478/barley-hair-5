Questions About Our Services

Learn more about our treatments and procedures

What hair restoration services does your hospital offer in China?
We offer comprehensive hair restoration services including hairline transplant, hair thickening, baldness restoration, eyebrow transplant, beard transplant, and body hair transplant. We also provide medical treatments like minoxidil and finasteride, as well as professional hair care programs to maintain your results.
Which is better for me: FUE or FUT hair transplant in China?
The best option depends on your specific needs and goals. Our advanced FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) offers minimal scarring and faster recovery, while FUT can provide more grafts in less time. During your free consultation, our expert doctors will evaluate your individual case and recommend the optimal approach for your situation.
Can women also benefit from hair transplant procedures in China?
Absolutely! Women experiencing hair loss can benefit from hair transplants, eyebrow transplants, and medical treatments. We offer customized treatment plans specifically designed for female pattern baldness and other hair loss conditions, with English-speaking staff available throughout.
Do you offer no-shave or long-hair hair transplant options in China?
Yes! We offer no-shave hair transplant options for maximum discretion. Our advanced long-hair FUE technique allows you to maintain your appearance throughout the process with minimal trimming of the donor area, perfect for international patients seeking privacy.
What is included in your professional hair care program in China?
Our professional hair care program includes personalized scalp treatments, nutrient therapy, laser therapy, and ongoing maintenance to support healthy hair growth and optimize your transplant results. This comprehensive approach ensures the best possible long-term outcome.
Can I combine hair transplant with medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride?
Yes! Many patients combine hair transplant surgery with medical treatments for optimal results. Our experienced doctors can create a comprehensive treatment plan that addresses both restoration of lost hair and prevention of further hair loss, tailored specifically to you.
Do you offer services specifically for international patients traveling to China?
Yes! We have a dedicated international patient coordinator who can help with travel arrangements, accommodations, and ensure your entire experience is smooth and comfortable. We also offer online consultations for patients who can't visit us in person first, with full English support available.
How can I learn more about your hair transplant services in China?
The best place to learn about all our services is right here on our official website and during your free consultation! We have detailed information about each treatment, and our experienced medical team can answer all your questions personally.
Do you offer free consultations before committing to any treatment in China?
Yes! All our consultations are completely free with no obligation. We believe you should have all the information you need to make an informed decision, and we're happy to take as much time as you need to answer all your questions and discuss your hair restoration goals.
Are your services available in multiple clinic locations?
Yes! We have over 30 direct-operated clinics in major cities across China. During your consultation, we'll help you find the most convenient location and ensure you receive the same high-quality care regardless of which clinic you visit.
