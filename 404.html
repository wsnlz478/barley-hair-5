our hospital Microneedle Implant Pen

Microneedle hair transplant, also known as implant pen technology, works by placing individual hair follicles into the hollow tip of the implant pen, then directly pushing the follicle into the pre-designed recipient area of the scalp. The pen can be rotated 360° to match natural hair growth direction, significantly reducing trauma and patient pain while avoiding secondary follicle damage.

Smaller incision (0.6-1.0mm) - 1/3 smaller
Minimal trauma, fast recovery
Wash hair in 24 hours
20-30% higher density
360° rotation for natural direction

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How soon can I see results after a hair transplant at our hospital?
You'll start seeing new hair growth within 3-4 months after your procedure. The transplanted hairs initially shed, which is completely normal, and then begin growing again. Full results typically take 9-12 months as the new hair continues to mature and thicken.
What should I expect during my first consultation?
During your free consultation, our specialist will examine your scalp and hair, discuss your goals, explain the procedure in detail, and create a personalized treatment plan. You can ask all the questions you like about the process, costs, and expected results.
Can women also get hair transplants at our hospital?
Yes! Hair transplants work effectively for women as well as men. We treat female pattern hair loss, thinning hairlines, and other hair loss concerns in women. Our experts will create a customized treatment plan specifically for your needs and goals.
Is a hair transplant painful at our hospital?
The procedure is very comfortable with our advanced techniques. We use local anesthesia to numb the treatment areas. Most patients report minimal to no discomfort during the procedure and can watch movies or listen to music to pass the time enjoyably.
What aftercare is required following a hair transplant?
We provide detailed aftercare instructions including how to wash your hair gently, when to return to normal activities, how to protect your scalp from sun exposure, and what medications or products to use or avoid. Our team is always available to answer any questions during your recovery.
Can I style my new hair normally after it grows?
Absolutely! Once your transplanted hair has fully grown, you can style it just like your natural hair - cut it, color it, and use any hair products you like. The transplanted hair behaves exactly like your natural hair because it is your natural hair!
What if I need multiple hair transplant sessions?
Some patients benefit from multiple sessions to achieve their desired density and coverage. Our doctors will discuss this with you during your initial consultation and create a long-term plan. We offer special packages for multiple sessions to make your journey more affordable.
How do I know if I'm a good candidate for hair transplant?
The best way to know is through a free consultation with our experts. Generally, good candidates have stable hair loss pattern, sufficient donor hair in the back and sides of the scalp, realistic expectations about results, and are in good overall health.
What precautions should I take before the procedure?
We'll give you specific pre-procedure instructions, which typically include avoiding certain medications and supplements, not smoking or drinking alcohol in the days leading up, avoiding excessive sun exposure, and making sure you get plenty of rest the night before.
Can I combine hair transplant with other treatments?
Yes! Many patients combine hair transplant with medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride to help maintain existing hair and improve overall results. We'll create a comprehensive treatment plan that may include multiple approaches for the best possible outcome.
