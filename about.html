Our Story & Mission

China Hair Transplant Hospital is the country's leading medical group specializing in premium hair restoration services. Founded in 2006, we pioneered microneedle hair transplant technology in China and have been setting industry standards ever since.

Our mission is simple: to help people from around the world regain their confidence through natural-looking, permanent hair restoration using our innovative microneedle technology.

With 30+ directly operated clinics in major Chinese cities, we provide comprehensive one-stop solutions including hair loss diagnosis, hair transplant surgery, medical hair care, and anti-hair loss treatments—all backed by our philosophy: "Good Doctors + Good Technology = Great Results."

Questions About Us

Learn more about our clinic and services

When was your hospital founded and what experience do you have in hair restoration?
Founded in 2006, we are the pioneer and leader of microneedle hair transplant technology in China. With over 18 years of specialized experience, we've successfully treated more than 100,000 patients from around the world and established 30+ direct-operated clinics across major Chinese cities including Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen.
What quality standards and certifications do your clinics maintain?
We maintain the highest international quality standards and follow rigorous protocols in all our procedures. With 10+ patented technologies, we combine Chinese precision with Western medical protocols to deliver world-class, natural-looking hair restoration results.
What cities in China have your hair transplant clinics?
We have 30+ direct-operated clinics across major Chinese metropolitan areas including Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Wuhan, Xi'an, and many more. All our clinics maintain identical high standards and use our innovative patented technologies.
What innovative technologies do you use for hair transplants?
We hold 10+ patented technologies, including our revolutionary microneedle implant pen that allows 360° rotation for perfectly natural hair direction. Our advanced microneedle technology uses ultra-fine 0.6-1.0mm incisions for minimal trauma, faster recovery, and 20-30% higher density results than traditional methods.
How many hair transplant procedures have you successfully completed?
We've successfully completed over 100,000 hair restoration procedures since our founding in 2006. Our diverse patient base includes people from over 50 countries worldwide who trust us for our surgical expertise, natural-looking results, and comprehensive aftercare support.
What makes your hospital different from other hair transplant clinics in China?
Our unique advantages include 18+ years of pioneering experience in microneedle technology, 30+ direct-operated clinics nationwide, 10+ patented innovations, international quality standards, dedicated English-speaking staff, and our revolutionary microneedle implant pen with 360° rotation capability.
How does hair transplant cost in China compare to Western countries?
Hair transplant cost in China is typically 50-70% less than in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, or Canada, while maintaining the same or higher quality standards. Our transparent pricing includes everything from consultation through aftercare, with no hidden fees.
What services do you provide specifically for international patients?
We've treated patients from over 50+ countries! Our services include full English-speaking staff, dedicated international patient coordinators, airport pickup assistance, comfortable accommodation recommendations, virtual follow-up consultations, and comprehensive travel guidance throughout your entire journey.
What safety measures and protocols do you have in place?
Patient safety and satisfaction are our absolute top priorities. We maintain the highest international standards in all our procedures with advanced sterilization protocols, experienced board-certified medical teams, comprehensive pre-operative evaluations, and thorough aftercare monitoring throughout your recovery.
How do I schedule a free consultation for hair transplant?
Scheduling is easy! All consultations are completely free with no obligation. You can contact us via WeChat, WhatsApp, email, or our website form. During your consultation, we'll evaluate your hair loss situation, discuss your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan tailored specifically for you.
