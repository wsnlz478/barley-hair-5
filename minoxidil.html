  • Widens the hair follicles

Proper Use & Results Timeline

Follow medical guidance for optimal results

Correct Application

  • Apply to DRY scalp only
  • 1mL twice daily (morning/night)
  • Spread evenly across thinning areas
  • Massage gently with fingertips
  • Allow 4 hours to dry before washing

Results Timeline

  • Month 1-2: Initial shedding (normal!)
  • Month 3-4: New growth begins
  • Month 6: Visible improvement
  • Month 12: Peak results
  • Continue use to maintain results

Safety & Considerations

  • Most people tolerate it well
  • Mild irritation possible initially
  • See doctor if irritation persists
  • Not for use during pregnancy/nursing
  • Must continue use for lasting benefit

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does minoxidil work for hair loss?
Minoxidil works by stimulating hair follicles to enter the anagen (growth) phase and prolonging it. It also increases blood flow to the hair follicles. This can help slow down or stop hair loss, and in some cases, help grow new hair.
How long does it take to see results from minoxidil?
You'll typically start seeing results in 3-6 months of consistent use. Maximum results are usually seen at 12-18 months. It's important to use minoxidil consistently every day for best results.
What happens if I stop using minoxidil?
If you stop using minoxidil, any hair that was gained or preserved because of the treatment will likely fall out over time. To maintain results, continued use is required.
Is minoxidil safe to use long-term?
Yes, minoxidil is generally considered safe for long-term use when used as directed. Most people tolerate it well. During your consultation, we'll monitor your progress and help ensure it's working well for you.
Can women use minoxidil?
Yes! Minoxidil is approved for use by both men and women. The concentration and application may be different, so it's important to use the product specifically recommended for your gender and situation.
Can minoxidil be combined with hair transplant surgery?
Yes! In fact, combining minoxidil with hair transplant surgery is often recommended. Minoxidil can help preserve your existing hair while the transplant restores what's been lost. We'll create a personalized plan for you.
What are the possible side effects of minoxidil?
Most people tolerate minoxidil well. The most common side effect is mild scalp irritation. Some people might notice increased hair growth on other parts of the face/body, which goes away if you stop. Serious side effects are rare.
Do I need a prescription for minoxidil?
In most cases, lower concentrations of minoxidil are available over-the-counter without a prescription. Higher concentrations or specific products might require one. During consultation, we'll help you choose the right product for your needs.
Can I use minoxidil if I color or style my hair as usual?
Yes! Just apply minoxidil to a clean, dry scalp and allow it to fully absorb before applying other products. We'll give you detailed instructions on how to incorporate minoxidil into your regular hair care routine.
What if I forget to apply minoxidil?
Don't worry! Just resume your regular schedule when you remember, but don't double up. Minoxidil works best with consistent daily use, but missing an occasional application won't ruin your progress.
