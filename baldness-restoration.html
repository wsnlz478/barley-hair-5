Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

Can all levels of baldness be treated with transplant?
Most cases of baldness can be treated, but it depends on having enough donor hair. During your consultation, we'll assess your donor supply and baldness pattern to determine what's achievable. In many cases, we can restore significant coverage even for advanced baldness.
How many grafts are typically needed for baldness restoration?
It varies greatly depending on the degree of baldness. For mild to moderate baldness, 1500-3000 grafts may be sufficient. For more advanced cases, 2500-5000+ grafts may be needed, sometimes in multiple sessions for optimal results.
Can I have multiple transplant sessions for severe baldness?
Yes! Many patients with severe baldness require multiple sessions to achieve their desired coverage and density. Sessions are typically spaced 6-12 months apart to allow for healing and to see how the transplanted hair grows before planning the next session.
What if I don't have enough donor hair for full restoration?
We'll work with you to create a realistic plan. We can prioritize restoring the most visible areas like the hairline and frontal scalp for maximum impact. We can also discuss combining transplant with medical treatments to prevent further loss and maximize your existing hair.
Can I use body hair for transplantation if scalp donor is limited?
In some cases, body hair can be used as an additional donor source, but scalp hair is always preferred. During your consultation, we'll assess all your options and recommend the best approach for your specific situation.
How long does it take to see full baldness restoration results?
You'll start seeing new hair growth at 3-4 months. Significant improvement will be visible at 6-9 months, and full, mature results at 12-18 months. For multiple sessions, the timeline extends accordingly.
Will I need to use medical treatments after baldness restoration?
We often recommend continuing medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride after transplant to prevent further loss of your native hair. This helps maintain the results we achieve with surgery. During consultation, we'll discuss what's best for your specific situation.
Is baldness restoration surgery painful?
No! We use local anesthesia to numb the donor and recipient areas completely. Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure and find it relatively comfortable. We'll make sure you're relaxed and comfortable throughout.
How long is recovery for baldness restoration?
Most patients can resume light activities and desk work within a few days. The transplanted area might have some redness or scabbing for the first week or so. We'll give you detailed aftercare instructions and a timeline for resuming all activities.
Can I choose between FUE and FUT for baldness restoration?
Yes! During consultation, we'll assess your situation and discuss which method would work best for you. FUE is great for minimal scarring and faster recovery, while FUT might be better for very large cases that need a lot of grafts.
