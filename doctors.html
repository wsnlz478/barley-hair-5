Questions About Our Doctors

Learn more about our medical team

What qualifications do our hospital's hair transplant doctors have?
Our doctors are highly qualified medical professionals with extensive training and experience in hair restoration surgery. They are trained in both Chinese and Western protocols, bringing together the best of both worlds for optimal patient outcomes.
Can international patients consult with English-speaking doctors at our hospital?
Yes! We have English-speaking doctors and staff available for consultations and throughout your entire treatment journey. Our international patient coordinator ensures clear communication and a smooth experience for patients from around the world.
How experienced are our hospital's doctors in hair transplant surgery?
Our medical team has extensive experience, with our clinic having completed over 100,000 successful procedures since 2006. Our doctors are trained in our patented microneedle technology and stay updated with the latest international advancements.
Will the same doctor handle my entire treatment process from start to finish?
Yes! Your primary doctor will be with you throughout your entire journey ï¿?from initial consultation and treatment planning, to the procedure itself, and ongoing follow-up care. This ensures continuity and personalized attention throughout.
What kind of training do our hospital's medical team members receive?
Our team undergoes rigorous training in our proprietary techniques and technologies. We combine Chinese precision with Western protocols, and our staff receives ongoing education to stay at the forefront of hair restoration advancements.
Can I choose which doctor will perform my hair transplant procedure?
During your consultation, you'll meet with our doctors who will evaluate your case. We'll match you with the doctor best suited for your specific needs and goals, ensuring you receive the best possible care and results.
Do our hospital's doctors stay updated with the latest international hair transplant advancements?
Yes! Our medical team regularly participates in international conferences, training, and continuous education to stay at the forefront of hair restoration technology and techniques. We're committed to bringing the world's best practices to our patients.
Can I find information about your doctors on Google or Bing?
You can find information about our clinic online, but the best place to learn about our doctors is right here on our website and during your free consultation! We're proud of our medical team and would love to introduce you to them!
What is your doctors' approach to patient care?
Our doctors take a personalized, patient-centric approach. They take time to listen to your goals, thoroughly evaluate your individual situation, and create a customized treatment plan that's right just for you. Your comfort, safety, and satisfaction are always our top priorities.
Are your doctors also trained in non-surgical hair loss treatments?
Yes! Our medical team is trained in both surgical and non-surgical hair restoration approaches. This means we can offer comprehensive treatment plans that may combine medical therapy with transplant surgery for optimal results.
