Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does no-shave FUE work?
Our no-shave FUE technique uses special tools and techniques that allow us to perform the transplant without requiring you to shave your entire head. This means you can go right back to your normal life without anyone knowing you've had a procedure.
Will anyone notice that I've had a hair transplant?
With our no-shave technique, most people won't notice! We only trim a tiny area in the donor zone, and the transplanted hairs are placed so they blend seamlessly with your existing hair. You can resume your social life immediately.
Is no-shave FUE more expensive?
While no-shave FUE does require more time and precision, the results are well worth it for many patients who value discretion. During your consultation, we'll discuss pricing and help you decide if it's the right choice for you.
Can I wear my hair long after no-shave FUE?
Yes! In fact, many patients who choose no-shave FUE have long hair, and the procedure is specifically designed to preserve your existing hairstyle. The tiny incisions heal quickly and are easily hidden by your existing hair.
How is recovery with no-shave FUE?
Recovery is very similar to regular FUE. Most patients can return to desk work within 2-3 days. The main difference is that you won't have a shaved head, so you'll feel more comfortable going out in public right away.
Is everyone a good candidate for no-shave FUE?
Most people are, but it depends on your specific situation, including your hairstyle, hair density, and the area being treated. During your consultation, we'll assess if you're a good candidate for no-shave FUE.
Does no-shave FUE affect the final results?
No! The final results are exactly the same as regular FUE. The only difference is that you get to keep your hair during the process. The transplanted hairs will grow just as naturally and permanently.
Can I style my hair normally right away?
Yes! While you need to be gentle with the transplanted area, you can style the rest of your hair as normal from day one. We'll show you exactly how to care for your new hair as it grows in.
What if my hair is short?
No-shave FUE can still work for shorter hairstyles! We have specialized techniques to minimize visibility. Come in for a free consultation and we'll show you what's possible for your specific hair length.
Can I get a no-shave transplant for facial hair?
Yes! No-shave techniques work beautifully for beard and eyebrow transplants too. This is especially popular for facial hair since there's no healing period with an awkward appearance.
