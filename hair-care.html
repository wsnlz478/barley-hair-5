Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What does a professional hair care program involve?
Our professional hair care programs include personalized scalp treatments, nutrient therapy, specialized shampoos and conditioners, laser therapy (when suitable), and ongoing maintenance plans tailored to your specific hair type and individual needs.
Can hair care help prevent hair loss?
Yes! A good professional hair care program maintains scalp health, strengthens existing hair, and in many cases slows or prevents further hair loss. When combined with medical treatments like minoxidil or finasteride, results can be even better.
How often should I get professional hair treatments?
It depends on your specific program and needs. Some treatments may be monthly, while others follow a different schedule. During your consultation, we'll recommend a personalized treatment timeline based on your unique hair goals.
Can I do professional hair care at home?
Yes! We provide professional-grade products and guidance on at-home hair care. While some treatments are best done in-clinic, we create a comprehensive plan that includes both professional clinic treatments and effective home care.
Do hair care programs work with hair transplants?
Yes! They work exceptionally well together. Professional hair care preserves your existing hair and optimizes scalp health, creating the ideal environment for transplanted hair to thrive and grow naturally.
Are professional hair care products better than store brands?
Professional-grade products typically have higher-quality ingredients, more effective formulations, and are specifically designed for optimal hair and scalp health. The best product depends on your hair type and needs. We'll recommend what's best for you.
How do I improve my hair's overall health?
Good hair health starts from within! Eat a balanced diet with sufficient protein, iron, and vitamins. Also avoid excessive heat and chemical treatments, keep your scalp clean, and protect hair from sun damage. We'll give you personalized tips during consultation.
What's the best way to wash my hair?
It depends on your hair type! Generally, use lukewarm (not hot) water, gently massage shampoo into your scalp, and condition mainly the ends. Washing frequency depends on your scalp—some need daily, others every few days. We'll give personalized advice.
Does diet really affect hair health?
Absolutely! Hair needs proper nutrition to grow healthy. Protein, iron, biotin, zinc, and vitamins D and B are all important. During consultation, we can discuss dietary factors and supplements that may benefit your specific situation.
What is low-level laser therapy for hair?
Low-level laser therapy (LLLT) uses lasers to stimulate hair follicles and potentially improve hair density. It's sometimes used alongside other treatments. During consultation, we can discuss if this would be beneficial for your specific situation.
