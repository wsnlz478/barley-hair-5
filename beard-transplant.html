Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does beard transplant work?
Beard transplant involves transplanting healthy hair follicles from the scalp donor area to the beard region. Using our microneedle technology, we place each follicle at precise angles and directions that match natural beard growth patterns for a completely natural appearance.
How many grafts are typically needed for a beard?
It varies depending on your goals and the area to be covered. For a full beard restoration, 800-3000 grafts may be needed. For filling in specific areas or creating a goatee or mustache, fewer grafts are required. We'll assess your needs during consultation.
Can you create a natural looking beard style?
Yes! Our doctors specialize in creating natural looking beard designs tailored to your face shape, existing facial hair, and personal preferences. We can create full beards, goatees, mustaches, or fill in patchy areas to achieve your desired look.
Will transplanted beard hair need to be trimmed?
Yes! Since the hair comes from the scalp, it will grow like scalp hair. You'll need to trim and maintain your transplanted beard just like natural facial hair to keep it at your desired length and style.
How long does it take to see beard transplant results?
You'll start seeing new growth at 3-4 months. Significant results will be visible at 6-9 months, and full, mature results at 12-18 months as the transplanted hairs continue to grow and establish.
Is beard transplant a painful procedure?
No! We use local anesthesia to numb the donor and recipient areas completely. Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure and find it relatively comfortable.
How long is recovery for beard transplant?
Most patients can resume light activities within a few days. The face might be a bit swollen or have some redness and scabbing for the first week or so. We'll give you detailed aftercare instructions and a timeline for returning to normal activities.
Can I shave after getting a beard transplant?
We'll give you specific instructions on when you can shave safely. Usually, you need to wait at least a couple of weeks to be gentle with the transplanted area. Once fully healed, you can shave just like you would with natural facial hair.
Can I choose what style of beard to get?
Yes! During consultation, we'll discuss your goals, assess your facial structure, and help design a beard style that's perfect for you. Whether you want a full beard, goatee, mustache, or just want to fill in patchy areas, we can create a custom plan.
Will the transplanted beard match my natural facial hair?
We do our best to match the texture and color as much as possible. Since the hair comes from the scalp, it will have scalp hair characteristics, but we carefully match grafts and place them to blend seamlessly. Over time, most people find it looks very natural.
