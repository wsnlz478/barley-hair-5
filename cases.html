Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

Are the before and after photos on your website real patient results?
Yes! All our before and after photos show real results from actual our hospital patients. We have a large portfolio of real patient transformations across various hair loss types and treatment approaches, demonstrating the natural results we achieve with our advanced technology.
Can I see before and after photos similar to my specific hair loss situation?
Absolutely! During your consultation, we can show you cases similar to your specific hair loss type, pattern, and goals. We have results from male and female patients, various degrees of baldness, and various treatment approaches.
How long does it take to see final hair transplant results?
You'll start seeing new hair growth at 3-4 months. Results continue to improve over time, with maximum density typically visible at 9-12 months. We provide a detailed timeline of what to expect at each stage during your consultation.
Do hair transplant results look completely natural?
Yes! Our 360Â° implant pen allows us to position each hair follicle at the exact angle and direction of natural growth pattern, creating completely natural-looking results that blend seamlessly with your existing hair.
Can I share my own before and after photos with our hospital?
We always appreciate when our patients sharing their journey! Many of our patients are happy to share their transformations to help others make informed decisions about hair restoration.
What types of hair restoration results can I expect from our hospital?
You can expect natural-looking, permanent results with excellent density. Our results demonstrate hairline transplant, hair thickening, beard transplant, and eyebrow transplant results ï¿?all achieving beautiful outcomes.
Will I need more than one session to achieve the results in your photos?
Some patients get amazing results in just one session, while others with more advanced hair loss choose to have multiple sessions for maximum density. During your consultation, we'll assess your situation and let you know what's realistic for your goals.
Are the results different for women compared to men?
We have amazing real results from both men and women! While the patterns of hair loss and treatment approaches can be different, we achieve beautiful, natural-looking results for all our patients regardless of gender.
Can I see results on Google or Bing?
You can find information about us on Google and Bing, but the best place to see our complete portfolio of real patient results is right here on our website and during your free consultation. We have thousands of before and after photos!
How soon after treatment will I look like the before and after photos?
Our before and after photos typically show results at 9-18 months after treatment. During your consultation, we'll walk you through the timeline and show you what to expect at each stage of the growth process.
