FUT Hair Transplant Technology Explained

Understanding how this traditional technique works

Strip Harvesting

  • Single strip of hair-bearing tissue removed
  • Typically 2-3cm wide in donor area
  • Closure with fine trichophytic technique
  • Minimizes scar appearance

Microscopic Dissection

  • Strip divided into individual follicular units
  • High precision under stereo microscopes
  • Preserves delicate follicles intact
  • Same high graft survival rate as FUE

Maximized Graft Yield

  • Excellent for large coverage requirements
  • More grafts available in single session
  • Cost-effective for extensive baldness
  • Perfect for Norwood 6-7 cases

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does FUT hair transplant work?
FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) involves removing a strip of tissue from the donor area (typically the back and sides of the head) and then dissecting it into individual follicular units that are then transplanted to the recipient area.
What are the advantages of FUT?
FUT is often preferred for very large cases that require many grafts. It can sometimes allow for higher graft density in a single session and may be more efficient for extensive hair restoration.
Will there be scarring with FUT?
FUT leaves a linear scar in the donor area. However, our advanced suture techniques minimize scarring as much as possible. The scar is typically hidden by your hair when it grows back.
How long is recovery with FUT?
Recovery with FUT is typically a bit longer than with FUE, but most patients can return to desk work within 3-5 days. Complete healing takes about 2 weeks. We'll provide detailed aftercare instructions.
How do I choose between FUE and FUT?
The choice depends on your specific needs. Factors like your hairstyle preferences, the number of grafts needed, and your recovery timeline all come into play. During your consultation, we'll recommend the best approach for your situation.
When will I see results from FUT?
Just like with FUE, you'll start seeing new hair growth at 3-4 months. Significant results at 6-9 months, and full, mature results at 12-18 months as the transplanted hair continues to grow and become thicker.
When will the stitches/sutures be removed?
Stitches are typically removed at 10-14 days after the procedure. We'll schedule this for you during your follow-up appointment. The stitches are small and dissolvable in some cases.
Can I still get a haircut after FUT?
Yes! Once healed, you can get haircuts as normal. Most patients keep their hair long enough to cover the donor scar anyway. We'll give you guidelines about when you can safely get a professional haircut.
Is FUT a better option for large cases?
FUT is often preferred for very large restorations because it can allow us to harvest more grafts in a single session while preserving donor density. During consultation, we'll help you decide what's best.
Can I have FUT if I've had previous scalp surgery?
In many cases yes! We'll assess your scalp during consultation. Sometimes we can work around previous scars, or use FUT in combination with other techniques. Come in for a free evaluation!
