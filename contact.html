Quick Questions

Answers to our most frequently asked questions

How do I book a free consultation?
Simply contact us via WeChat, WhatsApp, email, or fill out the form on this page. Our international patient coordinator will get back to you promptly to schedule your free consultation.
Do you offer online consultations?
Yes! We offer free online consultations for international patients who can't visit us in person. Contact us to schedule your virtual consultation with our doctors.
What cities in China have your clinics?
We have 30+ direct-operated clinics in major cities across China including Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Nanjing, and many more. We'll help you find the most convenient location.
Can you help with travel and accommodation?
Yes! We can assist international patients with airport transfers, hotel reservations, and planning their trip to China. We want to make your visit as smooth and comfortable as possible.
What languages do you speak?
We speak English fluently, and many of our staff also speak other languages. We'll ensure clear communication throughout your entire journey with us, from consultation to aftercare.
How long does it take to get a response?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. Our international patient coordinator is available via multiple platforms including WeChat, WhatsApp, and email for your convenience.
Is the consultation really free with no pressure?
Yes! All consultations are completely free and come with no pressure or obligation. We'll give you honest advice and answer all your questions, but it's completely up to you whether you decide to proceed with treatment.
What should I prepare for my first consultation?
Just come as you are! If you have photos of your hair from earlier years, those can be helpful. Also, if you've tried any treatments before, it's good to tell us about them. That's all you need to bring!

Do you have aftercare support after treatment?
Yes! We provide comprehensive aftercare support for all our patients, including follow-up appointments and guidance throughout your recovery process. We're with you every step of the way!
