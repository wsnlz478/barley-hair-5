Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What factors determine hair transplant cost in China?
Hair transplant cost depends primarily on the number of grafts required, the technique used (FUE, microneedle, FUT), and your specific treatment goals. Other considerations include the surgeon's experience, clinic quality, and any additional services included. We provide detailed, transparent quotes during your free consultation.
How many grafts will I need for my hair restoration?
Graft requirements vary: 500-1500 grafts for hairline refinement, 1500-3000 for moderate balding, 3000-4500 for extensive coverage, and 4500+ for severe hair loss (sometimes multiple sessions). During your free consultation, we'll conduct a detailed analysis and give you an exact graft count.
Do you offer payment plans or financing options?
Yes! We understand that hair transplant is an investment, and we offer flexible payment options to make treatment more accessible. During your consultation, we'll discuss the payment plans available and help find an option that works for you.
Is hair transplant covered by insurance?
In most cases, hair transplantation is considered an elective cosmetic procedure and is not covered by insurance. However, in rare cases where hair loss is due to a medical condition or trauma, coverage may be possible. Always check with your insurance provider.
Can I get a free consultation to discuss cost?
Yes! All consultations are completely free and come with no obligation. During your consultation, we'll assess your situation, discuss your goals, create a personalized treatment plan, and provide you with a transparent, detailed cost estimate.
Do you offer special packages for international patients?
Yes! We have special packages and dedicated support for our international patients traveling to us for treatment. We can assist with travel arrangements, accommodations, airport transfers, and more to make your journey as smooth and comfortable as possible.
Is there a price difference between FUE and FUT?
Yes, FUE is typically priced slightly higher because it's more time-consuming and requires specialized tools. However, many patients feel the benefits (no linear scar, faster recovery) are well worth it. We'll show you both options during consultation.
Do you charge per graft?
Yes, most hair transplant clinics use a per-graft pricing model because it's fair and transparent. This means you only pay for exactly what you need. During your free consultation, we'll calculate the exact number of grafts required for your goals.
Are there any hidden fees?
No! We believe in complete transparency. Your quote will include everything you need: the procedure itself, medications, aftercare products, and follow-up visits. What you see is what you pay鈥攏o surprises!
Is touch-up surgery included in the price?
Touch-up needs vary by patient. During your consultation, we'll discuss our policies and pricing for any future touch-ups you might want. Rest assured, we'll always be honest and upfront about all costs.
