Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

What hair loss treatments are available in China?
At China Hair Transplant Hospital, we provide a full spectrum of treatments including FDA-approved medications, advanced PRP therapy, professional scalp care, and hair transplant surgery. We'll develop a personalized treatment strategy during your initial consultation.
How to choose the best hair loss treatment?
Our specialists evaluate your unique hair loss pattern, scalp condition, health background, and personal objectives. This thorough assessment helps us recommend the most effective treatment or combination of treatments tailored specifically to you.
Can I use medical treatments and hair transplant together?
Absolutely! Combining treatments often yields the best results. Medical therapy helps maintain your existing hair while transplant surgery restores areas that have already thinned. We'll create a complete, integrated treatment plan for optimal outcomes.
How soon should I start treatment for hair loss?
Starting treatment as early as possible gives you the best chance to preserve more hair. If you're noticing increased shedding or thinning, schedule a consultation right away to explore your options and begin a personalized plan.
Do hair loss treatments work for both genders?
Yes, we offer effective treatment options for men and women. While specific approaches may vary, our goal is always the same: preserving existing hair and restoring what's been lost with customized plans for each patient.
What kind of results can I realistically expect?
Most patients experience slowed or halted hair loss with medical treatments, and permanent natural-looking hair restoration with transplant surgery. We'll discuss realistic expectations for your specific situation during your consultation.
Is it possible to prevent further hair loss?
While you can't change your genetics, early intervention can significantly slow or stop hereditary hair loss. A healthy lifestyle, proper nutrition, stress management, and avoiding harsh treatments also help maintain hair health. We'll discuss prevention strategies with you.
What if previous treatments haven't worked for me?
Don't worry! Many treatments work better in combination, or you may need a different approach altogether. We'll evaluate what you've tried before and create a new, personalized treatment plan specifically for your needs.
Could my hair loss indicate a health problem?
Most hair loss is genetic, but sudden or atypical hair loss can sometimes relate to underlying health conditions. We'll review your health history during consultation and help determine if additional medical evaluation would be beneficial.
Are professional hair care products helpful?
High-quality hair care products can support the health of your existing hair, though they can't restore lost hair. During consultation, we can recommend professional-grade products that complement your treatment regimen perfectly.
