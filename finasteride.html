How Finasteride Works

Understanding this powerful DHT-blocking treatment

Mechanism of Action

  • Inhibits 5-alpha-reductase type II enzyme
  • Reduces DHT by ~60% at 1mg dose
  • DHT is the main hormone causing baldness
  • Protects hair follicles from miniaturization
  • Oral, once-daily dosing

FDA-Approved for Men

  • FDA-approved in 1997 (Propecia®)
  • Clinically proven in multiple studies
  • ~90% see improvement or stability
  • For adult men only (18-41)
  • Gold standard oral treatment

Who It's For

  • Men with androgenetic alopecia
  • Early to moderate hair loss
  • Family history of balding
  • Pre-transplant stabilization
  • Post-transplant maintenance

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hair transplantation

How does finasteride work for hair loss?
Finasteride works by inhibiting 5-alpha-reductase, the enzyme that converts testosterone to DHT. DHT is the primary hormone responsible for male pattern baldness. By reducing DHT levels, finasteride helps slow down or stop hair loss and promote hair growth.
How long does it take to see results from finasteride?
You'll typically start seeing results in 3-6 months of consistent daily use. Maximum results are usually seen at 12-18 months. Like minoxidil, it's important to use finasteride consistently every day for best results.
What happens if I stop using finasteride?
If you stop using finasteride, any hair that was gained or preserved because of the treatment will likely fall out over time. To maintain results, continued daily use is required.
Can women use finasteride?
Finasteride is typically only recommended for men. Women who are pregnant or planning to become pregnant should not use finasteride at all. During your consultation, we'll discuss what treatments are safe and appropriate for you.
Is finasteride safe to use long-term?
Yes, finasteride is generally considered safe for long-term use when used as directed and under medical supervision. Most men tolerate it well. During your consultation, we'll discuss potential side effects and monitor your progress.
Can finasteride be combined with hair transplant surgery?
Yes! In fact, combining finasteride with hair transplant surgery is often recommended for optimal results. Finasteride can help preserve your existing hair while the transplant restores what's been lost. We'll create a personalized plan for you.
What are the possible side effects of finasteride?
Most men don't experience side effects, but the most common ones are related to sexual function. These are rare and typically reversible if you stop taking the medication. During consultation, we'll discuss potential side effects in detail.
Do I need a prescription for finasteride?
Yes, finasteride is a prescription medication because it requires medical supervision. During your consultation, we'll evaluate if you're a good candidate and discuss how to obtain a prescription if appropriate.
Can finasteride be combined with minoxidil?
Yes! Many men use both together because they work through different mechanisms. Finasteride blocks DHT, and minoxidil stimulates the follicles directly. This combination can often give better results than either alone.
What if I miss a dose?
If you miss a dose, just take it when you remember. If it's almost time for your next dose, skip the missed one and get back to your regular schedule. Don't double up! Consistency over time is what matters most.
