Microneedle Implant Pen

Our revolutionary patented technology

our hospital Microneedle Implant Pen

Microneedle hair transplant, also known as implant pen technology, works by placing individual hair follicles into the hollow tip of the implant pen, then directly pushing the follicle into the pre-designed recipient area of the scalp. The pen can be rotated 360° to match natural hair growth direction, significantly reducing trauma and patient pain while avoiding secondary follicle damage.

FUE Technology

FUE Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is a minimally invasive hair transplant technique that individually extracts hair follicles from the donor area using specialized tools. Our advanced FUE with microneedle implantation ensures precision placement and natural-looking results with minimal scarring.

Minimally invasive procedure

FUT Technology

FUT Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT) involves removing a strip of tissue from the donor area, then dissecting it into individual follicular units for transplantation. While still effective, our FUT procedures incorporate modern techniques for better healing and results.

Our Hair Transplant Process

Your complete 9-step journey to natural hair restoration

01

Consultation & Follicle Analysis

Professional scalp health assessment and hair loss grading. We evaluate your donor area follicle resources in the back of your head to determine your candidacy for hair transplantation.

02

Customized Treatment Design

Personalized hairline design considering your facial features and proportions. We hand-draw your hairline, plan the implantation zones, and calculate the exact number of follicles needed.

03

Pre-Operative Medical Check

Complete health screening including blood tests and coagulation assessment. We rule out any contraindications to ensure your procedure is safe and medically sound.

04

Local Anesthesia

Comfortable local anesthesia administration to both extraction and implantation areas. You'll remain awake and comfortable throughout the entire procedure with no pain.

05

Donor Follicle Extraction

Precision, scarless extraction of complete follicular units from the healthy donor area at the back of your head using specialized medical instruments.

06

Fine Follicle Separation

Medical staff carefully clean and sort high-quality follicles. Follicles are stored in temperature-controlled solution to maintain maximum viability.

07

Precision Follicle Implantation

Meticulous implantation of healthy follicles one by one using our patented microneedle pen. This precise technique ensures the highest possible survival rate.

08

Post-Procedure Dressing

Gentle cleaning of the scalp and application of sterile, non-restrictive dressings. We protect the implantation sites while minimizing friction and discomfort.

09

Aftercare Instructions

Comprehensive guidance on post-op hair washing, daily routine, and dietary restrictions. We provide clear recovery guidelines and schedule your follow-up appointments.

Questions About Our Technology

Learn more about our advanced hair transplant technologies

What is our hospital's revolutionary microneedle hair transplant technology?
Our patented microneedle technology uses tiny 0.6-1.0mm incisions and a revolutionary implant pen that allows 360° rotation for natural hair direction. This advanced technique results in minimal trauma, faster recovery, higher density, and more natural-looking results than traditional methods.
How does the 360° implant pen work and what are its benefits?
Our proprietary implant pen allows precise 360° rotation of each graft during placement. This ensures each hair follicle is positioned at the exact angle and direction of your natural hair growth pattern. The result is completely natural-looking hair that blends seamlessly.
What are the advantages of 0.6-1.0mm incisions compared to traditional methods?
Our ultra-fine 0.6-1.0mm incisions are significantly smaller than traditional hair transplant incisions. This means less trauma to the scalp, faster healing, minimal swelling and bruising, quicker return to normal activities, and the ability to achieve higher density results.
How soon can I wash my hair after our hospital's microneedle transplant?
With our advanced microneedle technology, most patients can wash their hair gently within 24 hours of the procedure. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and specialized products to ensure proper healing and optimal results.
What is the recovery time with our hospital's microneedle technology?
Recovery is significantly faster with our microneedle technology. Most patients can return to desk work within 2-3 days, and to more physical activities within 7-10 days. The tiny incisions heal quickly with minimal visible signs of the procedure.
Does our hospital's technology improve graft survival rates?
Yes! Our advanced technology and techniques ensure excellent graft survival. By minimizing trauma during extraction and placement, maintaining optimal graft storage conditions, and using cold storage and specialized handling, we achieve superior survival rates compared to traditional methods.
Is our hospital's technology patented?
Yes! Our microneedle technology and implant pen are patented innovations. These proprietary technologies were developed and refined in our clinics since 2006, representing thousands of hours of research and perfecting our techniques.
Can I find information about your technology on Google or Bing?
You can find general information about us online, but the best place to learn about our patented technology is right here on our website and during your free consultation! We'd love to walk you through how it works in detail.
Can your technology be used for both men and women?
Yes! Our microneedle technology works beautifully for both men and women. We customize the approach to each individual patient, regardless of gender, to achieve the most natural-looking and optimal results possible.
How does your technology compare to other international hair transplant methods?
We combine the precision of Chinese techniques with Western protocols and international best practices. Our technology is constantly refined based on the latest advancements, and we believe our results speak for themselves in terms of naturalness, density, and recovery time.
